Meet Tabitha

After twenty years of service in the United States Air Force, Tabitha returned to art and now tattoos full time. She specializes in delicate, meaningful pieces that mark someone you love, a memory, your faith, or a new beginning, and brings the patience and precision of her service to every appointment.

What I tattoo

Botanical · Fine Line · Script · Micro · Permanent Brows

Portfolio

Healed fine line work including botanical pieces, delicate script, desert and landscape designs, and small symbolic tattoos. Every piece is custom drawn.

Flash designs

Ready-to-book flash sheets of tiny tattoos are available to pick from, or they can be a starting point for something custom. Designs not shown can be added when you book.

Permanent brows

Fine-line permanent makeup brow work with natural hair-stroke detail, custom-mapped to your natural brow shape, growth pattern and face. First session $375, with a 30-day touch-up at $125 once everything has healed.

Booking

Appointments are requested through the booking page and are by appointment only. Clients must be 18 or older. New clients receive $25 off their first tattoo.
