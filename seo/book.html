Book an Appointment

Ready when you are.

Still Waters Fine Line Tattoo is by appointment only, in Peoria, Arizona. Send through your idea, placement, size and preferred dates and Tabitha will get back to you to arrange a time.

What to include

Your name and email, the style you are after, where on your body the piece is going, roughly how large you want it, the dates that suit you, and a short description of the idea.

Good to know

Clients must be 18 years of age or older. New clients receive $25 off their first tattoo. Tiny tattoo flash designs are pre-sized and ready to go, and a design that is not shown can still be included if you mention it when booking.
