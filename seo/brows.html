Your appointment

Each set starts by mapping your brows to your features and choosing a pigment that suits your skin and hair. The shape is then built in fine hair strokes, one at a time, so the result reads as naturally as possible. Brows settle over the weeks that follow, and a touch-up about 30 days later refines the shape and color once everything has healed.
